Add-on service

Cat litter waste collection, added to your regular visit

Got a cat as well as a dog? We'll take your bagged litter waste away at the same time as your garden clean — one visit, one less job for you.

How it works

Bag it, leave it out, we'll take it

Bag your used litter as normal and leave it out for your scheduled visit. We'll collect it alongside your garden clean and take it away with us — no separate call‑out needed.

🧾 £3 per bag, collected during an existing visit. For example, a standard weekly clean plus four bags of litter works out at £27 a week.

Currently available as an add‑on alongside a scheduled garden clean — message us if you'd like to check it can be added to your round.
