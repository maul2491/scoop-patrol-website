Frequently asked questions

Everything dog owners usually ask before their first clean. Can't find your answer? Message us directly.

How much does dog waste removal cost in Aberdare?

Prices start from £10 per clean for a small garden with one pet on a weekly round, rising with garden size, number of pets and whether you choose weekly or fortnightly. See our full pricing page for the complete breakdown.

How often should I have my garden cleared of dog poo?

Weekly is the most popular choice and keeps gardens consistently tidy and low‑odour. Fortnightly works well for smaller, single‑pet gardens where build‑up is slower, though each visit takes a little longer to clear.

Is dog poo bad for my lawn?

Left to sit, it can scorch grass and leave patchy, yellowed areas, and it's a common source of bacteria and parasites in gardens where children or other pets play. Regular removal is the simplest way to avoid this.

What happens to the waste you collect?

We remove it from your garden and dispose of it responsibly as a registered Upper Tier Waste Carrier with Natural Resources Wales (NRW) — fully compliant with current UK waste regulations.

Do you offer one-off cleans, or only recurring visits?

Both. Most customers settle into a weekly or fortnightly round, but we're happy to do a single one‑off clean, particularly for a big first tidy‑up before starting a regular schedule.

Do I need to be home during the visit?

No — as long as we can access the garden, you don't need to be in. Just let us know how to get in (side gate code, etc.) when you message us.

What areas do you cover?

Aberdare, Aberaman, Cwmaman and Mountain Ash, across the CF43, CF44 and CF45 postcode areas. Just outside that? Message us anyway — we're expanding the round regularly. See our areas we cover page for local details.

Do you collect cat litter as well as dog waste?

Yes, as an add‑on to a scheduled visit — £3 per bag collected alongside your garden clean. See our cat litter collection page for details.

Are your products safe for my pets and garden?

Yes — we use pet‑safe products and biodegradable bags throughout, kind to pets, plants and the wider environment.

How do I get a quote?

Message us your postcode, garden size and number of pets — a photo helps us give the fairest price, especially for larger gardens or first cleans. We'll come back with a price the same day.
